# Vehicle Purchase Procedures

## Buying process
The standard buying process involves selecting a vehicle from our inventory, completing a test drive, negotiating final pricing (including trade-ins or applied discounts), and finalizing the financial agreement with our finance department. The process can be started online but must be completed in person.

## Reservation process
High-demand or incoming factory-order vehicles can be reserved with a $500 refundable deposit. Reservations secure the vehicle for 48 hours after it arrives on the lot. If the customer does not finalize the purchase within that window, the vehicle is released to the general public.

## Required documents
To finalize a purchase, customers must provide:
* A valid state-issued driver's license.
* Proof of current auto insurance.
* Two recent pay stubs (if applying for dealer financing).
* Proof of address (utility bill or lease agreement) dated within the last 30 days.

## Registration
Our dealership handles all initial DMV registration paperwork and title transfers. Customers will be issued temporary paper tags valid for 45 days. Permanent metal plates and registration cards will be mailed directly to the customer's registered address by the DMV.

## Vehicle inspection
Every vehicle undergoes a mandatory 150-point safety and mechanical inspection before sale. A copy of the signed inspection certificate is provided in the final paperwork packet. Customers may also request a third-party mechanic inspection at their own expense prior to finalizing the sale.

## Delivery
Customers can take immediate delivery of on-lot vehicles once paperwork is signed. For custom orders or dealership trades, delivery is scheduled upon vehicle arrival. We offer complimentary home delivery within a 50-mile radius of the dealership for all new vehicle purchases.

## Taxes
All vehicle purchases are subject to state sales tax (calculated based on the customer's registered address, not the dealership's location) and local municipality taxes. These are calculated post-discount but pre-trade-in value deduction, in accordance with state law.

## Payment methods
We accept cashier's checks, certified bank checks, and direct wire transfers for full cash purchases. Personal checks are accepted but may delay vehicle delivery up to 5 business days for clearing. Credit cards are accepted for down payments up to a maximum of $5,000.

## Refund policy
We offer a 3-day or 150-mile (whichever comes first) "No Questions Asked" return policy on all used vehicles. The vehicle must be returned in the exact condition it was sold. New vehicles are final sale once driven off the lot and are subject to the manufacturer's warranty for defects.

## Order cancellation
Factory orders can be cancelled at any time before the vehicle enters the production queue for a full refund of the deposit. If cancelled after production begins, the $500 deposit becomes non-refundable.

## Frequently asked questions
* **Do I need an appointment to buy a car?** No, walk-ins are welcome, but appointments are recommended for faster service.
* **Can I buy a car entirely online?** You can complete 90% of the process online, but final signature and delivery require a physical presence.
* **Who handles my old loan if I trade in?** We pay off your existing loan directly to your lender and apply any positive equity to your new purchase.
