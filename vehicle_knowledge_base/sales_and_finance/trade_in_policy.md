# Trade-In Policies and Valuation

## Valuation process
All trade-ins are evaluated using real-time market data from the Manheim Auto Auction index, Kelley Blue Book (KBB) wholesale values, and current local inventory levels. Valuations take 20-30 minutes and require a physical appraisal by our used car manager.

## Guaranteed offer validity
Written trade-in offers are valid for 7 calendar days or 250 additional miles driven, whichever comes first. After this period, the vehicle must be reappraised.

## Accepted condition
We accept trade-ins in almost any condition, including vehicles that are not currently running ("push, pull, or drag" trades). However, vehicles with severe structural frame damage, deployed airbags, or branded/salvage titles will be assigned a flat salvage value (typically under $1,000).

## Negative equity (Underwater trades)
If you owe more on your current loan than the trade-in vehicle is worth, you have negative equity. We can often roll this negative equity into the new vehicle loan, provided your credit score supports the increased loan-to-value (LTV) ratio. Otherwise, the difference must be paid in cash at signing.

## Title requirements
To trade in a vehicle, the customer must be the registered owner. If the vehicle is paid off, the physical title must be present, free of liens, and signed by all parties listed on the title. If the vehicle still has an active loan, we require the lender's name and a 10-day payoff quote.

## Aftermarket modifications
Modifications (e.g., lift kits, custom paint, aftermarket exhaust systems) generally decrease the trade-in value, as they limit the potential resale audience. Customers are advised to return the vehicle to stock condition if possible to maximize trade-in value.

## Excluded vehicles
We do not accept the following vehicles for trade-in: motorcycles, ATVs, RVs, commercial heavy-duty trucks (Class 5 or above), or watercraft.
