# Discounts and Promotional Offers

## Military & Veteran Discount
* **Eligibility:** Active duty, reserve, and honorably discharged veterans of the Armed Forces.
* **Offer:** $500 cash rebate applied directly to the negotiated price of any new vehicle.
* **Required Documentation:** Leave and Earnings Statement (LES) or DD-214 form.

## First Responder Discount
* **Eligibility:** Active police officers, firefighters, EMTs, and paramedics.
* **Offer:** $500 cash rebate. Can be combined with standard promotional APR rates.
* **Required Documentation:** Valid department ID or state certification card.

## College Graduate Program
* **Eligibility:** Customers who have graduated from an accredited 4-year university or registered nursing program within the last 2 years, or will graduate within the next 6 months.
* **Offer:** $400 cash rebate and automatic approval for Tier 2 financing rates, regardless of limited credit history (provided there is no negative credit history).
* **Required Documentation:** Copy of diploma or final transcripts, plus a letter of employment commitment.

## Loyalty Bonus
* **Eligibility:** Current owners of a vehicle purchased from our dealership group.
* **Offer:** $750 trade-in bonus added to the appraised value of the vehicle, plus waiver of the dealership documentation fee.
* **Required Documentation:** Dealership records will verify prior purchase.

## EV Federal Tax Credit (Point of Sale)
* **Eligibility:** Buyers of qualifying Electric Vehicles (e.g., VoltZen Pure EV) who fall below federal income thresholds ($150,000 for single filers, $300,000 for joint filers).
* **Offer:** Up to $7,500 point-of-sale rebate applied immediately as a down payment.
* **Required Documentation:** IRS Form 15400 filled out at the time of sale.

## Stacking rules
The Military, First Responder, and College Graduate discounts **cannot** be stacked together (a customer qualifying for multiple must choose one). However, any of these demographic discounts *can* be stacked with the Loyalty Bonus and the EV Federal Tax Credit.
