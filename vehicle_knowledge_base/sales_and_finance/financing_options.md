# Financing and Loan Options

## Loan periods
We offer standard auto loans spanning 24, 36, 48, 60, 72, and 84 months. Longer loan periods lower the monthly payment but increase the total interest paid over the life of the loan.

## APR
Annual Percentage Rates (APR) are tiered based on credit scores.
* **Tier 1 (720+):** 1.9% - 3.5%
* **Tier 2 (680-719):** 4.0% - 5.5%
* **Tier 3 (620-679):** 6.5% - 8.5%
* **Tier 4 (Under 620):** 9.0%+ (Subject to secondary lender approval)

## Down payments
While zero-down financing is available for Tier 1 buyers, we generally require a minimum 10% down payment for new vehicles and 15% for used vehicles to secure favorable interest rates and prevent the customer from immediately being "underwater" on the loan.

## Eligibility
Financing eligibility requires a minimum credit score of 580, no active bankruptcies, and a debt-to-income (DTI) ratio not exceeding 45% (inclusive of the projected new auto loan payment).

## Required income
Applicants must demonstrate a minimum gross monthly income of $2,500. For loans exceeding $40,000, a minimum gross monthly income of $4,000 is required. Income is verified via W2s, pay stubs, or two years of tax returns for self-employed individuals.

## Early settlement
All of our standard auto loans are simple-interest loans with no prepayment penalties. Customers can make principal-only payments or pay off the entire loan balance early without incurring any extra fees.

## Late payment penalties
A grace period of 10 days is provided after the due date. Payments received on day 11 or later are subject to a late fee equal to 5% of the past-due payment amount or $35, whichever is greater.

## Balloon payments
We offer commercial balloon payment structures where customers pay smaller monthly installments for 36 months, followed by a large final "balloon" payment for the remaining principal. This is only available for commercial fleet buyers.

## Leasing vs financing
* **Financing:** You own the vehicle after all payments are made. No mileage limits. Customizations are allowed. Higher monthly payments.
* **Leasing:** You are renting the vehicle for a set term (usually 24-36 months). strict mileage limits apply (typically 10,000 to 12,000 miles/year). Must be returned in factory condition. Lower monthly payments.
