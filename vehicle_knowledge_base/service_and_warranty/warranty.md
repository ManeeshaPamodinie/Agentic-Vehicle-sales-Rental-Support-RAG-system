## Vehicle Warranty Coverage

### Basic warranty
Our comprehensive "Bumper-to-Bumper" basic warranty covers defects in materials or workmanship for 3 years or 36,000 miles, whichever comes first. This covers all vehicle systems excluding routine maintenance items.

### Powertrain warranty
The powertrain warranty covers the engine block, transmission, drive systems, and internal lubricated parts for 5 years or 60,000 miles.

### Hybrid battery warranty
For hybrid models, the high-voltage battery and associated hybrid control modules are warrantied for 8 years or 100,000 miles.

### EV battery warranty
Electric vehicle high-voltage battery packs are warrantied for 8 years or 100,000 miles. This warranty includes a degradation guarantee, ensuring the battery will retain at least 80% of its original total capacity for the duration of the warranty.

### Roadside assistance
Complimentary 24/7 roadside assistance is included for the duration of the basic warranty (3 years/36,000 miles). This covers towing to the nearest certified service center, flat tire changes, jump-starts, and lockout services.

### Exclusions
The warranty does not cover normal wear and tear items (e.g., brake pads, wiper blades, cabin filters), damage from environmental factors (hail, floods), or failures caused by the installation of aftermarket performance parts.

### Transfer policy
All remaining warranty coverage is fully transferable to subsequent owners. A $50 administrative transfer fee applies and must be registered with our corporate office within 30 days of the vehicle's title transfer.
