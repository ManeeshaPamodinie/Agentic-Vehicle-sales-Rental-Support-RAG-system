## Recommended Maintenance Schedule

### Recommended intervals
Standard vehicle maintenance should be performed every 6 months or 5,000 miles, whichever comes first. Vehicles operated in severe conditions (heavy towing, extreme dust, constant short trips) should adhere to a 3-month or 3,000-mile interval.

### Oil changes
* **Conventional/Synthetic Blend:** Replace every 5,000 miles.
* **Full Synthetic:** Replace every 10,000 miles (filter must still be inspected at 5k intervals).

### Tire rotation
Tires should be rotated every 5,000 to 7,500 miles to ensure even tread wear. For AWD vehicles, it is critical to rotate every 5,000 miles to prevent drivetrain stress from uneven tire circumferences.

### Brake inspections
A comprehensive inspection of brake pads, rotors, calipers, and brake fluid lines should occur every 10,000 miles. Brake fluid should be flushed and replaced every 30,000 miles or 3 years.

### Coolant
Engine coolant (and EV thermal management fluid) should be inspected every 15,000 miles. A complete system flush and replacement is required every 50,000 miles or 5 years.

### Transmission service
* **Automatic Transmissions:** Fluid flush and filter replacement required every 60,000 miles.
* **eCVT / EV Gearboxes:** Generally sealed for life, but fluid condition should be inspected at the 100,000-mile mark.
