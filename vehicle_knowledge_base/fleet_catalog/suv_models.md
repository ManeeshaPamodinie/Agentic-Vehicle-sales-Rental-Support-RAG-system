# TerraQuest X7 SUV

## Vehicle overview
The TerraQuest X7 is a rugged yet refined full-sized three-row SUV engineered for adventure, towing capability, and deep wilderness exploration. Featuring high ground clearance and an intelligent terrain management setup, it accommodates large families who refuse to compromise on utility.

## Engine
3.5L Twin-Turbocharged V6 engine.

## Transmission
10-speed heavy-duty automatic transmission with selectable tow/haul modes.

## Fuel economy
17 MPG City / 23 MPG Highway / 19 MPG Combined.

## Horsepower
380 hp @ 5,500 rpm.

## Drivetrain
Intelligent 4-Wheel Drive (4WD) with electronic locking rear differential.

## Seating capacity
7 to 8 passengers (depending on second-row captain's chair configuration).

## Cargo capacity
20.8 cubic feet behind the third row; expands to 84.3 cubic feet with all rear seats folded flat.

## Safety features
* Trailer sway control and integrated trailer brake controller
* Front and rear parking sensors with automatic reverse braking
* 360-degree panoramic view monitor with terrain view modes
* Traffic sign recognition and driver drowsiness alert

## Technology features
* 14.0-inch ultra-wide high-definition multimedia display
* Multi-zone rear seat entertainment system with dual 10.1-inch screens
* 14-speaker Lexicon premium surround sound system
* Wi-Fi hotspot capability for up to 5 devices

## Standard equipment
* 20-inch rugged all-season alloy wheels
* Panoramic sunroof with power sunshade
* Genuine leather-trimmed first and second-row seating
* Power-folding third-row seating

## Optional packages
* **Off-Road Adventure Package:** Adds underbody skid plates, all-terrain tires, tuned off-road shocks, and a crawl control mode.
* **Luxury Executive Package:** Replaces the second-row bench with heated/ventilated captain's chairs and adds a rear center console.

## Available colours
* Granite Gray Metallic
* Sahara Sand Gold
* Forest Green Mica
* Shadow Black Matte

## Warranty eligibility
Eligible for the TerraQuest Rugged Protection Plan: 5-year/60,000-mile bumper-to-bumper warranty and 7-year/100,000-mile powertrain coverage.

## Financing eligibility
Eligible for standard financing terms up to 72 months; balloon financing options available for commercial use.

## Recommended customer profile
Large families, outdoor adventurers, boat/camper owners needing high towing capacity, and drivers requiring true off-road performance.

Limited availability. 3 units currently on-site due to high regional demand; custom orders carry a 4 to 6-week factory fulfillment timeline.
