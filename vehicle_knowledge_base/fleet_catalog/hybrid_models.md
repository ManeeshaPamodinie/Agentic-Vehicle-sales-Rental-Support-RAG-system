# EcoPulse H2 Hybrid

## Vehicle overview
The EcoPulse H2 is a highly efficient compact crossover hybrid that perfectly balances an ultra-low carbon footprint with everyday versatility. Utilizing a self-charging parallel hybrid system, it offers exceptional city mileage and rapid torque response, making it the quintessential urban runabout.

## Engine
1.8L 4-Cylinder Atkinson-cycle engine paired with a high-output electric motor.

## Transmission
Electronically controlled Continuously Variable Transmission (eCVT).

## Fuel economy
52 MPG City / 49 MPG Highway / 51 MPG Combined.

## Horsepower
139 hp (combined system net horsepower).

## Drivetrain
Electronic All-Wheel Drive (AWD-e) with on-demand rear motor deployment.

## Seating capacity
5 passengers.

## Cargo capacity
22.5 cubic feet of rear cargo space with an adjustable load floor.

## Safety features
* Proactive driving assist with gentle braking curves
* Lane tracing assist and road sign detection
* Low-speed backup collision avoidance system
* Safe exit assist to prevent door-opening accidents into cyclists

## Technology features
* 8.0-inch floating infotainment screen
* Cloud-based navigation with real-time traffic updates
* Over-the-air (OTA) software update capability
* Smart Key system with push-button start and digital key access

## Standard equipment
* 16-inch aerodynamic wheels with low-rolling-resistance tires
* Bi-LED projector headlights with automatic high beams
* Premium fabric seats made from recycled ocean plastics
* 7.0-inch digital gauge cluster display
