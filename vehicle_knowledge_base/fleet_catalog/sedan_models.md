# Apex Aura Sedan

## Vehicle overview
The Apex Aura is a premium, mid-sized family sedan designed to blend executive styling with everyday practicality. It offers a smooth ride, whisper-quiet cabin tracking, and advanced digital integration, making it a top contender for suburban commuters and small families.

## Engine
2.0L Turbocharged Inline 4-Cylinder engine with direct injection.

## Transmission
8-speed electronically controlled automatic transmission with sport mode and paddle shifters.

## Fuel economy
28 MPG City / 37 MPG Highway / 32 MPG Combined.

## Horsepower
241 hp @ 5,200 rpm.

## Drivetrain
Front-Wheel Drive (FWD) with active traction control.

## Seating capacity
5 adults.

## Cargo capacity
16.5 cubic feet of trunk space with 60/40 split-folding rear seats.

## Safety features
* Pre-collision braking with pedestrian detection
* Lane departure alert with steering assist
* Blind-spot monitoring with rear cross-traffic alert
* Adaptive cruise control with stop-and-go functionality

## Technology features
* 10.2-inch central touchscreen infotainment display
* Wireless Apple CarPlay and Android Auto compatibility

## Standard equipment
