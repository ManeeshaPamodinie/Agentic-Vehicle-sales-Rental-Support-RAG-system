# VoltZen Pure EV

## Vehicle overview
The VoltZen Pure EV is a cutting-edge, long-range battery electric vehicle (BEV) built on a dedicated next-generation skateboard architecture. Delivering instantaneous acceleration, an entirely flat cabin floor, and ultra-fast DC charging capabilities, it represents the zero-emission future of the fleet.

## Engine
Dual high-efficiency permanent magnet synchronous AC electric motors (Front and Rear).

## Transmission
Single-speed direct-drive reduction gearbox.

## Fuel economy
118 MPGe City / 102 MPGe Highway (Target range of 310 miles on a full charge).

## Horsepower
320 hp.

## Drivetrain
Dual-Motor All-Wheel Drive (AWD) with real-time torque vectoring.

## Seating capacity
5 adults.

## Cargo capacity
24.1 cubic feet in the rear trunk plus a 2.3 cubic foot front trunk ("frunk").

## Safety features
* Autonomous emergency steering assist
* Intersection turn collision mitigation system
* Remote smart parking assist (vehicle parking itself from the key fob)
* Under-battery armored shield architecture for impact protection

## Technology features
* 15.4-inch continuous curved glass command console
* Augmented reality (AR) heads-up display mapping onto the windshield
* Premium 16-speaker spatial audio audio layout
* Phone-as-a-Key setup via ultra-wideband (UWB) wireless protocol

## Standard equipment
* 19-inch aero-optimized alloy wheels
* Heat pump system for optimized winter cabin range preservation
* 100% vegan leather interior upholstery
* 8-way power adjustable ventilated front seats

## Optional packages
* **Performance Boost Package:** Unlocks software-controlled motor curves lowering 0-60 mph times, adding 20-inch wheels and performance brakes.
* **Autonomous Driving Beta Tier:** Unlocks highway lane-change automation and navigate-on-autopilot features.
