## Rental Insurance and Protections

### Standard Liability (Included)
All rentals include state-mandated minimum liability coverage. This covers damage to third-party property or individuals up to the state minimum limits, but does not cover the rental vehicle itself or the renter's personal injuries.

### Collision Damage Waiver (CDW)
Available for $22/day. This is not technical insurance, but a waiver that absolves the renter of financial responsibility for theft or physical damage to the rental vehicle, provided the rental agreement was not violated.
* **Deductible:** $0 out-of-pocket for covered incidents.

### Supplemental Liability Insurance (SLI)
Available for $15/day. This extends the standard third-party liability coverage up to $1,000,000. It protects the renter against claims made by a third party for bodily injury or property damage caused during the rental.

### Personal Accident Insurance (PAI)
Available for $7/day. Provides accidental death and medical expense benefits to the renter and passengers for injuries sustained in an accident while inside the rental vehicle.

### Insurance Exclusions
No insurance package covers damage caused by off-roading, driving under the influence of alcohol or drugs, using the wrong fuel type, or allowing an unauthorized driver to operate the vehicle.
