## Driver and Qualification Requirements

### Valid License
The primary renter and any additional drivers must present a valid, unexpired government-issued driver's license. The license must be in good standing and held for a minimum of one uninterrupted year prior to the rental date. Digital licenses are not accepted at pickup.

### International Driving Permit
Renters with driver's licenses issued outside the United States or Canada must present their home country's physical license along with a valid International Driving Permit (IDP). Passports are also required for identity verification.

### Credit Card Requirements
A major credit card (Visa, MasterCard, American Express, or Discover) in the primary renter's exact name must be presented at pickup. Prepaid cards, virtual cards, and debit cards are not accepted for the security deposit hold, though debit cards may be used for final payment upon return.

### Background and Driving Record
We reserve the right to verify driving records. Rentals will be denied to individuals whose records show a DUI/DWI conviction within the past 48 months, three or more moving violations within the past 24 months, or instances of driving with a suspended license.
