## Rental Policies and Procedures

### Driver age
The minimum age to rent a vehicle is 21. Drivers between the ages of 21 and 24 are subject to a Young Renter Fee of $25 per day and are restricted from renting luxury vehicles, full-size SUVs, or commercial vans.

### Security deposit
A temporary hold is placed on the primary driver's credit card at the time of pickup. The standard deposit is $500 for economy and mid-size vehicles, and $1,000 for luxury, EV, and full-size SUV rentals. This hold is released upon safe return of the vehicle.

### Mileage limits
Standard daily and weekly rentals include 150 miles per day. Any mileage driven beyond the cumulative limit is billed at $0.25 per mile upon return. Unlimited mileage packages can be purchased upfront for an additional $15 per day.

### Fuel policy
We operate on a "Full-to-Full" fuel policy. Vehicles are provided with a full tank of gas (or 90%+ charge for EVs) and must be returned at the same level. If returned with less fuel, a refueling fee of $8.50 per gallon (or $0.50 per kWh) applies.

### Late returns
We offer a 1-hour grace period from the scheduled return time. Vehicles returned 1 to 2 hours late incur a half-day charge. Vehicles returned more than 2 hours late are billed for an entire additional rental day.

### Cleaning fees
Vehicles must be returned in reasonably clean condition. A $150 detailing fee is applied if the vehicle requires excessive interior cleaning (e.g., mud, pet hair, food stains). We enforce a strict non-smoking policy; any evidence of smoking incurs a flat $250 sanitization fee.

### One-way rentals
One-way rentals are permitted between our certified branch locations. A one-way drop fee applies, ranging from $75 to $200 depending on the distance between the origin and destination branches.

### Extensions
Rental extensions must be requested at least 24 hours prior to the original drop-off time. Extensions are subject to vehicle availability and will be billed at the current daily rate, which may differ from the original booked rate.

### Cross-border travel
Vehicles must remain within the continental United States. Cross-border travel into Canada or Mexico is strictly prohibited and immediately voids all insurance coverages and rental agreements.

### Accident reporting
In the event of an accident, the driver must first contact local authorities (police) to file an incident report. Immediately after, the driver must contact our 24/7 Rental Emergency Hotline to report the damage and arrange for a replacement vehicle.
